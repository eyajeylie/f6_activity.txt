Version control acts as the ultimate safety net and collaboration engine, allowing multiple team members to work on the same project simultaneously without overwriting each other's contributions. It maintains a detailed, chronological history of every modification, making it easy to track changes, understand project evolution, and seamlessly merge updates.
Git tracks every single change automatically in a single file without cluttering your folders. It provides a complete, searchable history of your project, allowing you to see exactly who changed what and when. Most importantly, Git lets you safely test new ideas in isolated "branches" and merge them back later, eliminating the risk of accidentally destroying your working copy.
